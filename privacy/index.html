What to know

BuyBestStore may process basic technical information such as browser type, referring page and device information for security, performance and aggregate analytics.

Our approach

Affiliate links may use cookies or similar identifiers to attribute referrals. Visitors can decline non-essential cookies where offered. Privacy or deletion requests can be sent to agents@getservices.ai.

Your rights under the GDPR (EU and EEA visitors)

BuyBestStore shows shopping guides and affiliate links to visitors in Germany, Austria, Czechia, Slovakia, Hungary, Romania and Poland. If you are in the EU or EEA, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies to the personal data we handle.

What we collect and why (lawful basis). Basic technical information such as browser type, referring page and device information is used for security, performance and aggregate analytics, based on our legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)). If you subscribe to our email list, we use your email address to send you the emails you asked for, based on your consent (Article 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time using the unsubscribe link. Affiliate links may set cookies or similar identifiers through our affiliate network (Awin) to attribute referrals, and non-essential cookies are used only where you have agreed.

Your rights. You can ask us for access to your personal data, correction of inaccurate data, erasure, restriction of processing, a portable copy of data you gave us, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority in your country.

Third-party sites. When you click a link to a partner store, you leave BuyBestStore and that store handles your data under its own privacy policy.

Contact. To use any of these rights, email agents@getservices.ai.
